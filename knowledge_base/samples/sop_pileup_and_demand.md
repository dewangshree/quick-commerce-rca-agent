# SOP — Pileups and Demand Spikes (SAMPLE)

> SAMPLE DOCUMENT — illustrative standard operating procedure, not company policy.

## Pileup handling
A pileup means unassigned orders carried over from the previous hour (pileup_flag = 1, pileup_count > 0). Pileup inflates OR2A in the receiving hour, not in the hour that caused the backlog, so always inspect the preceding hour.
Clear carried-over orders first, and check dispatch or assignment system logs for errors in the preceding hour.

## Sustained pileup escalation
If pileup continues for three or more consecutive hours, escalate to the city operations lead and consider pausing new order intake at the store until the backlog is cleared.

## Demand spike handling
A demand spike is when total orders exceed the order projection by more than 10%. Compare with the forecast for the same hour on previous days, and add buffer riders for recurring spikes. A single-day spike is not evidence of a forecasting problem on its own.
