# Historical Incident — STORE_017 evening pileup (SAMPLE)

> SAMPLE DOCUMENT — fictional incident written for demonstration. The store code and figures are invented and are not from the loaded dataset.

## Summary
On a fictional date in March, the store saw a sustained pileup from 19:00 to 22:00. Breach rate for the evening was in the red band.

## Observed signals
Pileup flagged for four consecutive hours. Booking was above 90% but utilization (man_hour) dropped below 0.85 with several no-shows in the Night Slot.

## Contributing factors identified
Utilization gap driven by rider no-shows, followed by a sustained pileup. Demand was within 10% of projection.

## Actions taken
Fleet team contacted riders with repeated no-shows; an evening standby rider pool was trialled.

## Lessons
Check no-shows as soon as a pileup starts; utilization gaps compound into sustained pileups.
