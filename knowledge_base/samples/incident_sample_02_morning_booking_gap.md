# Historical Incident — STORE_044 morning booking gap (SAMPLE)

> SAMPLE DOCUMENT — fictional incident written for demonstration. Not real company data.

## Summary
A fictional morning where peak-slot bookings were well below capacity and OR2A rose across 08:00–11:00.

## Observed signals
current_capacity_booked between 70% and 80% for Morning Slot 2 and 3. No pileup in the first hour; pileup appeared from the second hour onwards. Orders were slightly above projection.

## Contributing factors identified
Booking gap in morning peak slots, with a secondary demand spike. Pileup was a consequence of under-supply rather than an independent factor.

## Actions taken
Morning slots were opened two days in advance and incentives for the first morning slot were reviewed.

## Lessons
When a booking gap and pileup co-occur, check which started first before naming the pileup as a contributing factor.
